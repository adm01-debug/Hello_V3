# On-Call Guide

## Responsibilities
- Monitor alerts
- Respond to incidents
- Escalate when needed
- Document incidents

## Alert Severity
- **P0 (Critical):** System down, respond immediately
- **P1 (High):** Major feature broken, respond within 1h
- **P2 (Medium):** Minor issue, respond within 4h
- **P3 (Low):** Enhancement, handle during business hours

## Response Procedure
1. Acknowledge alert
2. Assess impact
3. Communicate status
4. Investigate and fix
5. Document resolution

## Escalation
- P0: Escalate to senior engineer immediately
- P1: Escalate after 30 minutes if unresolved
- P2: Escalate after 2 hours if unresolved
